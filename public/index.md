# 2240 Speed Shop | Classic Restoration & Restomods, Edmonton

HTML: https://2240speedshop.com/

2240 Speed Shop is Terry Harmider's Edmonton shop on the Sherwood Park line. Restorations, restomods, LS swaps, body and paint for Alberta classics and trucks.

## Work

- [Restoration](/services/classic-car-restoration.md) — frame-off or rolling, honest scope, real hours.
- [Restomods & hot rods](/services/restomods-custom-builds.md) — period looks, current running gear.
- [Engine swaps & builds](/services/engine-swaps-builds.md) — LS and diesel conversions built to start at minus thirty.
- [Performance & tuning](/services/classic-performance-tuning.md) — carb rebuilds and ignition, jetted for Alberta air.
- [Body, paint & metal](/services/body-paint-metalwork.md) — rust repair, patch panels, floor pans, paint.
- [Interiors & classic service](/services/classic-interiors-service.md) — period-correct materials, mechanical service.
