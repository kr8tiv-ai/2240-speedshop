# Terry Harmider, Edmonton Shop Owner

HTML: https://2240speedshop.com/about/

Terry Harmider owns and runs 2240 Speed Shop, the customs-and-classics garage at 4507 82 Ave NW in east Edmonton. The steel sign, the D100, the Radium run.

This page is about the person who runs the bay, not a corporate profile. Quotes and walk-arounds go through Terry.
